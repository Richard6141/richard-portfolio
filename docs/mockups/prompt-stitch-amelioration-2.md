## ✅ PROMPT (EN — recommandé)

```
Refine the landing page further. Keep the same brand and content, but push the design noticeably further. The current version is correct but too "safe" and standard — I want more creative personality. All interface text stays in FRENCH. Apply ALL of the following.

1) BE MORE CREATIVE & DISTINCTIVE (most important):
   - Make it memorable, not a generic template. Use your own artistic judgment to add tasteful, original design flourishes: bolder typographic scale and contrast, more dynamic and asymmetric compositions, layered elements and depth, creative section transitions/dividers, expressive (but controlled) use of the accent color, refined micro-details.
   - Surprise me with a strong, cohesive creative direction — while staying professional and credible. Avoid clichés and anything that looks AI-generated.

2) LOGO (image provided): use the uploaded logo image as the brand mark. Place it top-left in the header, correctly sized and vertically centered, and again in the footer. Keep clear space around it. Do not stretch or distort it.

3) "RÉALISATIONS" SECTION — change the approach: do NOT feature the "robot that prospects 500 craftsmen/month" as the single flagship case study (that metric should no longer dominate the page). Instead, design a flexible PORTFOLIO/réalisations section: a clean gallery/grid of project cards (each with a title, a category tag like "Site web", "Application", "Automatisation", a short result line, and room for a thumbnail). Include 3–6 project slots and allow elegant "Projet à venir / Bientôt publié" placeholder cards, since several projects are not public yet. The section must look great even with few items. Keep the page anchored on SERVICES and value, not on one number.

4) TESTIMONIALS — redesign this section with richer, fuller cards ("cartes bien garnies"): each card has a client avatar/photo, full name, role + company, a star rating, a longer and more substantial quote, and a small tag for the service used (e.g. "Site e-commerce", "Automatisation"). Consider a more designed layout — e.g. one larger featured testimonial plus smaller ones, or a carousel — with clear visual hierarchy and a quotation accent. Make it feel premium and trustworthy.

5) FOOTER — make it premium and complete: the logo + a short tagline, a clear multi-column structure (Services, Liens utiles, Contact with email/phone/Cotonou Bénin), social links, a short "Disponible pour vos projets" call-to-action or mini contact prompt, legal links (Mentions légales, Confidentialité), language switch if relevant, and "© 2026 Richard Salanon". Balanced spacing, no empty feel.

Keep everything responsive and add the smooth Apple-style scroll animations from before (staggered reveals, count-ups, subtle parallax, a pinned scroll moment). Deliver the full refined page.
```
